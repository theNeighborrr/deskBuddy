# Roadmap and decisions

## Working now — 0.5.0

Companion with seven animated characters, clock, city weather, Wi-Fi signal, phone settings, and signed wireless updates with recovery. Weather Cloud reacts to conditions within the single Companion page.

## Next decisions

- Review the new characters on the actual display and refine their appearance.
- Choose a product name; DeskBuddy remains provisional.
- Make first-time setup and recipient instructions ready for a small gift batch.
- Extend testing from short bench runs to everyday use.

## Deferred

Countdown is intentionally postponed. Do not treat it as part of the current release.

## Potential apps — ideas, not commitments

- Focus timer, Pomodoro, daily facts, and reminders.
- Market watch and currency exchange.
- Home lab status: server uptime, Jellyfin, and download activity.
- GitHub activity and a read-only mission-control page.
- Network diagnostics, device presence, and connectivity alerts.
- ADS-B aircraft, Meshtastic, and vessel tracking.
- Lighting and other home integrations.

These will need a decision about which data lives on-device and which integrations need a companion service. There is no downloadable community app catalog yet. The longer-term idea is a small personalized gift batch and an extensible desk display.
