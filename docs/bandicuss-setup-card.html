YOUR DESK DISPLAY // 0.7

BANDICUSS

Neon Arcade · Characters · Clock · Weather

PLUG IN. TAP IN.

Connect USB power and leave the top untouched for two seconds. Neon Arcade is your first page. If a setup screen appears, tap once to enjoy the artwork offline.

CONNECT FOR CLOCK + WEATHER

  1. Hold the top for five seconds to show setup.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi shown on the display using its password. Stay connected if your phone says “no internet.”
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 in your browser. Connect the display to your home 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  4. Choose your timezone, weather city, and units, then tap Save my settings.

THE TOP IS YOUR CONTROL

Tap: next page
Hold 1 second: back to Neon Arcade
Hold 5 seconds: phone settings

To change settings later, use the address and code shown on the display while your phone is on the same home Wi-Fi. All six pages and characters are customizable. Setup trouble? Restart, keep the top clear, and try again. Clock needs internet after power loss; artwork works offline.
